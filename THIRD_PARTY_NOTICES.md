## Wallet logos (`apps/web/public/wallets/`)

Copied by `scripts/wallet-logos.py` from the icon each wallet ships in its
`@solana/wallet-adapter-*` package (https://github.com/anza-xyz/wallet-adapter,
Apache-2.0): Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, Bitget Wallet, Coinbase Wallet,
Trust Wallet (shield only, on a white tile), Magic Eden, Exodus, Nightly,
Coin98, TokenPocket and Ledger. The OKX mark comes from Simple Icons (CC0).
Each logo is a trademark of its wallet provider and is shown only so people
can pick their wallet.
