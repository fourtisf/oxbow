## Body

Every cycle on Solana tells the same story. By the time a token is all over your timeline, the wallets that found it early are already taking profit.

Oxbow closes that gap. It watches the wallets that keep winning, shows you every buy and sell they make seconds after it lands on chain, and lets you copy any trade in one tap, straight from your own wallet.

It's live now at oxbowflow.com.

**Heading:** What Oxbow does

**A live feed of smart money.** Every trade from tracked wallets as it happens: the token, the size, the price paid and the USD value. Filter by size, or switch to only the wallets you follow.

**One-tap copy trading.** See a buy you like, tap Copy, pick an amount and approve it in your wallet. Trades route through Jupiter for the best price across Solana, pump.fun and PumpSwap included.

**Wallet pages that show the full picture.** A 30-day leaderboard ranked by realized profit. For every wallet: realized and unrealized PnL, win rate, current holdings checked against the chain, and every closed position.

**Token pages with a safety check.** Price, market cap, liquidity, holders and age, plus a check of mint and freeze authority, holder concentration, developer share, organic trading and Jupiter verification. You also see which tracked wallets hold it.

**Order flow.** The latest fills from every trader in a token, grouped by price, so buy and sell pressure is visible at a glance.

**Limit orders.** Buy lower or sell higher with Jupiter limit orders, right from the trade panel.

**Alerts.** A browser notification the moment a wallet you follow trades. No account needed.

**Heading:** How we find smart money

"Smart money" is easy to claim and hard to prove. Most wallets that look profitable aren't worth copying, so Oxbow does the filtering for you.

- It watches who is trading right now: on Jupiter, on pump.fun and PumpSwap, in the busiest tokens, and above all the wallets that got into winning trades early.
- Every candidate is judged on its own recent history, read straight from the chain.
- Bots are thrown out: wallets whose transactions mostly fail, that trade faster than any person could, or that sandwich other traders.
- Wash-trading farms are thrown out: groups of wallets churning fresh copycat tokens nobody else trades. Their profits look real on paper; copying them means buying rugs.
- What remains has to earn: closing trades at a profit consistently, or landing a large realized win in tokens with real trading behind them.

Every wallet is checked again daily. The ones that stop earning, or start acting like bots, are dropped.

Have a wallet you already watch? Paste its address and Oxbow tracks it too.

**Heading:** Your keys, your coins

Oxbow is non-custodial. We never hold your funds and never ask for your seed phrase. Oxbow builds the transaction, your wallet shows it to you, and nothing moves unless you approve it.

Before your wallet even opens, Oxbow simulates the trade against your real balance. If it would fail, because the price moved past your slippage or you're short on SOL, you're told why and nothing is sent.

Oxbow's fee is a flat 0.5% of the SOL side of each trade, always shown before you sign.

Works with Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and the other major Solana wallets.
