Overview

A pool for any token, priced in a stock

Oxbow creates a liquidity pool that pairs a token already on Solana with a tokenized stock, SOL or USDC. Traders buy your token with the pair asset, and the pool sets the price automatically from what's inside it.

You don't launch a new token. You bring an existing mint, choose what to pair it with, deposit both sides and pick whether the liquidity is burned, locked or left unlocked.

Pricing

Drag to see a trade move the price

Every pool holds two reserves. A buy takes your token out and puts the stock in, so each next token costs a little more. The product of the two reserves stays constant.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to own the token's mint?

No. Anyone holding a token can create a pool for it. Buyers see who created each pool and who holds its liquidity.

Which stocks can I pair with?

Any stock token on the verified list. Oxbow only lists stock tokens whose issuer publishes a price feed and that have enough liquidity to route trades.

Can I take my liquidity back?

If you left it unlocked, yes, any time. If you locked it, when the lock ends. If you burned it, never. In every case you keep collecting trading fees.

Where does my pool trade?

On the venue you picked: Raydium, Meteora or Orca. Aggregators like Jupiter route trades into it automatically.
